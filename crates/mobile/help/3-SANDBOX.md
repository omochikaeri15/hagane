# Sandbox
The `Sandbox` page runs a battle with the game's own battle logic on your device, using the game files the app has imported. It has four tabs and a Play button.

## Lineup
Swipe in from the **left edge** (or tap an empty cell) to open the unit roster, then tap a unit to add it to the lineup. Hold a unit briefly and drag it to another cell to move it, or drag it off the lineup to remove it. Tap a unit to open it and change its form, level and talents; its orbs sit under the talents in that header. `Lineups` keeps several saved lineups.

The combos below the lineup are the ones your fielded units take part in. Double tap a combo to move its units onto the top row.

## Stage
The `Stage` tab is the Stages page. The stage and crown you pick there is the one the sandbox plays. Zombie Outbreaks and the Filibuster Invasion are entered the way the game enters them.

## Config
`Config` holds the battle settings, the treasure levels, the base tech levels, the Cat Base parts, the battle items, the invasion stages and the tutorial flags. `Quality` lowers the resolution the battle is drawn at, which makes it faster on a slow phone.

## Replay
Every battle is recorded as the latest battle unless `Settings > Sandbox > Disable Replays` is on. The `Replay` tab lists the latest battle and every replay saved to the `sandbox` folder. `Save to Library` keeps the latest battle there, `Export to BCV` writes it as a replay file through your export location, and `Watch` plays it back. A replay recorded in another game version asks before it plays.

## Battle
Tap and drag like in the game. Pinch to zoom the battlefield. While a replay plays, hold a finger on the screen for two seconds to leave it, or double tap to open the live readout of every unit's health and the current seed. If the battle faults, you can continue past the fault or terminate the battle.
