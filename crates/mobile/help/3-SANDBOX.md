# Sandbox
The `Sandbox` page runs a battle with the game's own battle logic on your device, using the game files the app has imported. It has four tabs and a Play button.

## Lineup
Swipe in from the **left edge** (or tap an empty cell) to open the unit roster, then tap a unit to add it to the lineup. Hold a unit briefly and drag it to another cell to move it, or drag it off the lineup to remove it. Tap a unit to open it and change its form, level and talents; its orbs sit under the talents in that header. `Lineups` keeps several saved lineups.

The combos below the lineup are the ones your fielded units take part in. Double tap a combo to move its units onto the top row.

## Stage
The `Stage` tab is the Stages page. The stage and crown you pick there is the one the sandbox plays. Zombie Outbreaks and the Filibuster Invasion are entered the way the game enters them.

## Config
`Config` holds the battle settings, the treasure levels, the base tech levels, the Cat Base parts, the battle items, the invasion stages and the tutorial flags. `Quality` lowers the resolution the battle is drawn at, which makes it faster on a slow phone.

## Replay
Every battle is recorded as the latest battle unless `Settings > Sandbox > Disable Replays` is on. The `Replay` tab lists the latest battle and every replay saved to the `sandbox` folder. `Save to Library` keeps the latest battle there, `Export to BCV` writes it as a replay file through your export location, and `Watch` plays it back. A replay recorded in another game version asks before it plays.

`Settings > Sandbox > Auto-Save BCV` saves every battle to the `sandbox` folder as you leave it, and `Saved BCV Name` picks what those files get called.

Swipe right on the `Replay` tab to open the list of replays. `Latest Battle` sits above it, and replays can be organized into sub-folders there. `Create Folder` makes one inside the selected folder, and its name is edited in the name field like a replay's. Tap a folder to open it. Folders go up to 4 levels deep, and anything deeper is left out of the list. Press and hold a replay or folder for a moment to pick it up, then drag it onto another folder, or onto an empty spot to take it back out. A drag without the hold scrolls the list. `Delete Folder` deletes the selected folder and every replay inside it, and asks you to tap it again to continue.

## Battle
Tap and drag like in the game. Pinch to zoom the battlefield. While a replay plays, hold a finger on the screen for two seconds to leave it, or double tap to open the live readout of every unit's health and the current seed. If the battle faults, you can continue past the fault or terminate the battle.

## Roguelike
This mode has special behaviors specific to it. There are "Bonuses" as well as "Registration" that can be attached to units.

Selecting a Roguelike map causes you to automatically enter Roguelike mode. In this mode: lineup behavior changes, the available cats on the cat list narrows, and many configuration options are disabled.

The linup in this mode is replace-only. Dragging units outside of the lineup does not remove them and dragging units ontop the linup swaps them.

Provided within the lineup are two buttons: "Global Bonus" and "Cat Bonus." They are self explanitory: one is for the global bonuses that applies to groups of cats or other attributes, and the other is for cat-sepcific bonuses.

To "register" a cat on a lineup, click and hold on their Deploy Icon for 0.7 seconds. Registered cats get a +5 boost to plus level as well as a point drop boost for the respective event stages.

The lineups made under each Roguelike map is also contained to that specific map. This is due to each map having a unique roster as well as unique ability-changing mechanics detatched from the regular game.

There are limits that the game enforces for recruits and bonuses. Provided under the cat list and within the bonus popups are counters for these limits. If you go over a limit for the cat list, the offending cats will be outlined in red. Seperately, going over a bonus limit will cause the buttons that open the bonus popups to become red. When you exceed a limit, "Play" is disabled.
