# General
Hagane keeps everything it works on inside one folder on your device that you can reach with any file manager.

## Files
On Android the folder is `Android/data/app.omochikaeri.hagane/files` on your internal storage. Inside it you will find:
- **game**: the game files the app reads. Fill it through the `Import` page.
- **mods**: one folder per mod, exactly as the desktop app lays them out.
- **exports**: statblocks, animations, backgrounds, packs and archives the app writes for you. With `Settings > General > Exports` set to `Ask Where` (the default on Android) the system's save dialog opens for every finished export so you choose where it goes; `App Folder` keeps everything here.
- **sandbox**: the replays you save from the `Sandbox` page's Replay tab.

The Files tab in `Settings` shows how much space the game files and caches take and lets you clear them.

## Navigation
- Swipe in from the **right edge**, or tap the arrow in the top right corner, to open the page list.
- Swipe in from the **left edge** on the Cats, Enemies, Stages, Mods and Files pages to open their lists.
- In the animation viewer, **drag** to pan and **pinch** to zoom. The corner button expands the viewer over the whole screen.
- Tap an ability icon to read its name. Tap it again to close the tip.
- Popups can be dragged around by their title bar, the same way they can on desktop.
